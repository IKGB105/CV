\cvsection{Key Projects}

\projtitle{IoT Safety Checklist \& Hardware Interlock System}
\projtech{Raspberry Pi / Node.js / Shelly Smart Relay}
\projlink{https://github.com/IKGB105/IOT-Safety-Checklist}{IKGB105/IOT-Safety-Checklist}
\projdesc{Production system deployed at a food manufacturing plant. Digitizes pre-shift machine safety checklists and physically interlocks machine startup — a smart relay wired into the start-button circuit only closes after the checklist is approved. Runs fully offline as its own WiFi access point, web server, and REST API, with photo evidence capture, automated WhatsApp alerts, and an audited supervisor-override flow. Strongest evidence of full-stack integration: hardware, firmware, and software, actually running, not a class exercise.}
\vspace{1.5pt}

\projtitle{ARM Processor PCB Design}
\projtech{KiCad, 2-Layer PCB, ARM Cortex-M (QFP)}
\projlink{https://github.com/IKGB105/PCB-ARM-Kicad}{IKGB105/PCB-ARM-Kicad}
\projdesc{Designed and routed a 2-layer PCB from schematic capture through fabrication-ready Gerbers and drill files, around an ARM Cortex-M MCU with USB power/programming, user I/O, and expansion headers for BOOT mode selection and a camera interface. Used a ground pour across the stackup to keep return paths short without a dedicated internal ground plane.}
\vspace{1.5pt}

\projtitle{Adjustable LM317 Power Supply — SPICE, PCB \& Thermal FEM}
\projtech{LTspice, KiCad, COMSOL Multiphysics}
\projlink{https://github.com/IKGB105/LM317-Adjustable-PowerSupply}{IKGB105/LM317-Adjustable-PowerSupply}
\projdesc{End-to-end power supply design documented as an engineering logbook: LTspice simulation of the adjustable topology, KiCad PCB layout, and a COMSOL thermal FEM study of the regulator's worst-case junction temperature that drove two rounds of heatsink selection. Simulation caught a real 76\,mV arithmetic error in an earlier hand-calculation before it propagated further. Companion LM2596 buck converter root-caused its thermal limit to the catch diode, not the regulator.}
\vspace{1.5pt}

\projtitle{IK-Dron --- 5" FPV Racing Drone}
\projtech{BetaFlight, SpeedyBee F405 V4}
\projlink{https://github.com/IKGB105/IK-Dron}{IKGB105/IK-Dron}
\projdesc{Team project: diagnosed and flew a 5" racing drone after its flight controller suffered an unrecoverable hardware failure. Migrated to a SpeedyBee F405 V4 stack on BetaFlight, then root-caused a takeoff failure via the CLI to a propeller/motor-rotation mismatch tripping BetaFlight's runaway-takeoff protection.}
